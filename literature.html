Editorial: About ProQuant

  • An editorial giving an overview of the rationale for pattern 4 quantification
    Vickers AJ, Fine S; ProQuant Group. Evaluating Tumor Quantification in Place of Proportions in Prostate Cancer: Principles of the ProQuant Group. Eur Urol. 2025 Dec 18:S0302-2838(25)04835-3. doi: 10.1016/j.eururo.2025.11.013. Epub ahead of print. PMID: 41419390.

Key papers demonstrating prognostic value of pattern 4 quantification

  • Pattern 4 quantification of biopsy cores is superior to Gleason score and multivariable models for predicting distant metastasis after radical prostatectomy in patients with biopsy grade group 2 - 4
    Olivier J, Vertosick E, Delobel L, Bommelaere T, Puech P, Leroy X, Vickers A, Villers A. Quantification of prostate cancer Gleason pattern 4 to predict oncological outcome. BJU Int. 2025 Aug 7. doi: 10.1111/bju.16884. PMID: 40774801.

  • In patients with biopsy grade group 2 – 4 undergoing radical prostatectomy, mm of pattern 4 on biopsy better predictor of advanced surgical pathology and biochemical recurrence than either grade group or a multivariable model of standard clinical predictors
    Pickersgill NA, Fletcher SA, Aiken N, Assel MJ, Liso N, Reuter VE, Vickers AJ, Ehdaie B, Fine SW. Total Gleason Pattern 4 Length Outperforms Grade Group and Clinical Models in Predicting Oncologic Outcomes in Grade Group 2-4 Prostate Cancer. medRxiv [Preprint]. 2026 Mar 2:2026.02.27.26347289. doi: 10.64898/2026.02.27.26347289. PMID: 41867176; PMCID: PMC13001378.

  • Patients with grade group 2 – 4 with <2mm of pattern 4 on biopsy have equivalent risk to those in the favorable intermediate risk category - and hence are eligible for active surveillance – but constitute a group that is twice as large
    Fletcher SA, Pickersgill NA, Aiken N, Assel MJ, Liso N, Reuter VE, Ehdaie B, Fine SW, Vickers AJ. Gleason pattern 4 length and active surveillance eligibility in Grade Group 2-4 prostate cancer. BJU Int. 2026 Oct 4. doi: 10.1111/bju.70478. Epub ahead of print. PMID: 42830647.

  • In patients with grade group 2 prostate cancer, length of pattern 4 in biopsy cores predicts risk of adverse pathology at surgery (often considered to be the first high-quality paper to examine pattern 4 quantification)
    Dean LW, Assel M, Sjoberg DD, et al. Clinical Usefulness of Total Length of Gleason Pattern 4 on Biopsy in Men with Grade Group 2 Prostate Cancer. J Urol. 2019;202(2):309-316. doi:10.1016/j.juro.2018.07.062

  • In a large radiotherapy cohort of grade group 2 and 3 patients, strongest predictor of distant progression and prostate cancer-specific mortality was percentage of biopsy tissue that was pattern 4, equivalent to mm pattern 4 adjusted for number of cores
    Delahunt B, Steigler A, Atkinson C, Christie D, Duchesne G, Egevad L, Joseph D, Kenwright DN, Matthews J, Murray JD, Oldmeadow C, Samaratunga H, Spry NA, Thunders MC, Hondermarck H, Denham JW. Percentage grade 4 tumour predicts outcome for prostate adenocarcinoma in needle biopsies from patients with advanced disease: 10-year data from the TROG 03.04 RADAR trial. Pathology. 2022 Feb;54(1):49-54. doi: 10.1016/j.pathol.2021.11.004. Epub 2021 Dec 24. PMID: 34955242.

Significance of the Percentage of Gleason Pattern 4 at Prostate Biopsy in Predicting Adverse Pathology on Radical Prostatectomy: Application in Active Surveillance.   • In surgery patients with biopsy grade group 2, there is a greater risk of advanced pathology in patients with higher mm of pattern 4, but small sample size and limited statistical analysis focusing on cut-points precludes further conclusions
    Sato S, Kimura T, Onuma H, Fukuda Y, Egawa S, Takahashi H. Pathol Int. 2020 Dec;70(12):992-998. doi: 10.1111/pin.13026. Epub 2020 Sep 30. PMID: 32997878.

  • In surgery patients with grade group 2, only highest percentage of pattern 4 and not tumor length predicts advanced pathology, but endpoint definition included grade group, entailing a circular argument

Findings best explained by pattern 4 volume being prognostic

  • Tumor volume assessed on MRI predicts 7-year risk of metastasis after surgery or radiotherapy (MRI volume reflects amount of pattern 4)
    Yang DD, Lee LK, Tsui JMG, Leeman JE, McClure HM, Sudhyadhom A, Guthier CV, Taplin ME, Trinh QD, Mouw KW, Martin NE, Orio PF, Nguyen PL, D’Amico AV, Shin KY, Lee KN, King MT. AI-derived Tumor Volume from Multiparametric MRI and Outcomes in Localized Prostate Cancer. Radiology. 2024 Oct;313(1):e240041. doi: 10.1148/radiol.240041. PMID: 39470422.

  • Numerous papers have found if grade is discordant between settings (e.g. targeted and systematic biopsy, or between biopsy and surgical pathology), risk is intermediate between the two grades. This is best explained in terms of pattern 4 volume. The larger the tumor (and hence for a given Gleason score, the larger the amount of pattern 4), the more likely sampling will be adequate and the less likely it is to find discordant Gleason scores
